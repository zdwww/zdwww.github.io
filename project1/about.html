● 张三 Plate I

Plate I · 临清狮猫 · The Linqing Lion-Cat

张三, quiet & immaculate

A 临清狮猫 — the rare Linqing Lion-Cat from Shandong: pure white, long-haired, with pink ears, a pink nose, and a plume tail that arrives in the room slightly after the rest of her.

张三 carries a placeholder name with great dignity. In Chinese, “张三” is the standard stand-in for any unnamed person — the everyman. She has made it her own. She does not announce herself; she simply appears, already composed, already exactly where she meant to be.

Her preferred operating mode is “observing.” The other two run a small, ongoing business of nuisance and noise; 张三 files quarterly reports.

● Peanut Plate II

Plate II · Devon Rex · The Curly Ginger

Peanut, warm & relentless

A Devon Rex with a ginger curly coat, a pale belly, ears like satellite dishes, and a face that suggests she is always about to ask a question.

Peanut considers most surfaces to be either “hers” or “temporarily hers.” She performs the duties of a small orange sun: she occupies laps, she rolls over for inspection, she announces her arrival by the simple fact of being arrived. A paper bag is, to her, a worthy adversary.

She is, in technical terms, easily delighted. Stringed objects, ankles, the rustle of any wrapper, and reflections of all kinds are filed under Items Worth Investigating.

● Mabu Plate III

Plate III · Norwegian Forest Cat · The Silver Tabby

Mabu, fluffy & theatrical

A Norwegian Forest Cat — long-haired silver tabby with a white chest, white socks, tufted ears, and a tail held like a flag in still wind.

Mabu supervises, mostly from a high vantage point, and has strong opinions about which shelf belongs to whom. Her preferred furniture is whatever a human is currently sitting on, edged out only by the rule that windowsills outrank everything else.

In photographs she favors a three-quarter profile and tends to look just past the lens, as if something more interesting is happening behind the photographer.
